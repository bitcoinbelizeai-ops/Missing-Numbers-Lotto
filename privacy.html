Privacy Policy

Missing Numbers · Last updated 22 November 2026

The short version

  • Accounts are free and optional. Everything on the site works without one.
  • An account keeps your Lucky Numbers on every device you sign in on.
  • We collect as little as possible, never sell it, and never show your email or first name to anyone.
  • You can delete your account at any time from the Account screen.
  • You must be 18 or older to create an account.

What we keep and where

Without an account, nothing about you leaves your phone. Your settings and Lucky Numbers are saved only on your own device.

With an account, we keep these details in our database, which is run for us by Supabase, an online database service:

  • your email address (to send your sign-in code)
  • your nickname, and your first name if you choose to give it
  • your age range and district
  • whether you ticked the news box, and which version of these terms you agreed to
  • when you joined and when you last opened the site
  • your saved Lucky Numbers

The site itself is hosted on GitHub Pages, and its fonts come from Google Fonts. Like any website, these services see your device's internet address when pages load. They don't get your account details.

To count visits, we use Cloudflare Web Analytics. It tells us things like how many people visited, which pages they opened, and roughly which country they were in. It uses no cookies, doesn't follow you to other websites, and doesn't tell us who you are.

Emails

We only email you your sign-in code. If you tick the news box, we may also send occasional site news; you can stop it any time by contacting us.

On your phone

The site saves a few settings on your own device (your game, sound, Easy reading and Lucky Numbers) so it opens the way you left it. If you sign in, it also keeps you signed in. We don't use advertising or tracking cookies.

Keeping it safe

  • Everything is sent over an encrypted connection.
  • Database rules let each person reach only their own account.
  • There are no passwords to steal: you sign in with a one-time code sent to your email. Keep your email account secure, because anyone who can read it can sign in.

How long we keep it

  • Sign-ups that never enter their code are removed after 24 hours; unfinished accounts after 7 days.
  • Accounts not used for 2 years are deleted, with a reminder email 30 days before. Opening the site while signed in counts as using it.
  • When you delete your account, or we delete it for breaking our Terms of Service, your profile and saved Lucky Numbers are removed straight away.

Your rights

You can delete your account yourself from the Account screen. For a copy of your data or anything else, contact us and we'll answer within 30 days.

Want the details? Read Belize's Data Protection Act, 2021 (official copy from the National Assembly of Belize).

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. Using the site after that means you accept the new version.
