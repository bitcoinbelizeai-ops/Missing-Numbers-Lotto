  3. Repository access: Only select repositories → Missing-Numbers-Lotto.
  4. Permissions → Repository permissions → Contents: Read and write. Nothing else.
  5. Click Generate token, copy it, paste it here.

BBN & Love FM (Facebook)

How BBN and Love FM work

Results read from BBN Belize's Facebook posts. A good result for a draw the site doesn't have yet is added to the site automatically within about a minute. It never replaces a result already on the site: anything that doesn't fit stays here with "Add to site", and disagreements show in red at the top. Love FM's Facebook text posts are a second source for Boledo only, with the same rules; their rows say "Love FM". magayo is the backup and double-checks results overnight.

    Last 7 days on the site

    Results posted here reach phones within about 1 to 10 minutes (GitHub keeps the file cached for up to 10 minutes). The automatic job skips any draw already posted, so it saves a magayo call.

    Public holidays

    No Boledo, Pick 3 or Fantasy 5 draw on these days. Members see the greeting in the app ("No draw today · Happy Independence Day! 🇧🇿"), and the results jobs save "no draw" automatically. Use the dates the government observes (some holidays move to a Monday). The yearly list is on pressoffice.gov.bz.

    Backups & restore

    Download everything that is on the site. Keep a backup somewhere safe (email it to yourself, or save it to a USB stick).

    Restore a backup

    Choose a backup file. Nothing changes on the site until you check what it will do and press Restore.

    Before anything changes, a safety copy of what is on the site now is saved to this device, and you can press Undo right after.
