The short version

  • Accounts are free and optional. Everything on the site works without one.
  • An account keeps your Lucky Numbers on every device you sign in on.
  • We collect as little as possible, never sell it, and never show your email or first name to anyone.
  • You can download your data or delete your account at any time from the Account screen.
  • You must be 18 or older to create an account.

We don't collect your address, phone number, ID, payment details or location. If you tick the news box, we may email you occasional site news; untick it any time by contacting us.

Where it's kept

Account data is stored with Supabase (servers in the United States), and sign-in emails are sent through Resend. That means your information is kept outside Belize, with providers that protect it under contract. Access is limited by database rules so each person can only reach their own information.

How long we keep it

  • Sign-ups that never enter their code are removed after 24 hours; unfinished accounts after 7 days.
  • Accounts with no sign-in for 2 years are deleted, with a reminder email first.
  • When you delete your account, your profile and saved Lucky Numbers are removed straight away.

Your rights

Under Belize's Data Protection Act 2021 you can ask to see, correct, download or delete your information, or object to how it's used. Most of this you can do yourself from the Account screen. For anything else, contact us and we'll answer within 30 days.

Terms of use

  • Missing Numbers is an information site. It is not affiliated with BGL or any game operator, and it does not sell tickets or take bets.
  • Results are gathered from public sources and may contain mistakes. Always check results with the official operator.
  • Statistics such as missing or hot numbers describe past draws only. Every draw is random; nothing here predicts future results.
  • Choose a nickname that isn't your real name and isn't offensive. We may change or remove nicknames that break this rule, and suspend accounts that misuse the site.
  • Keep your email account secure: anyone who can read your email can sign in to your Missing Numbers account.
  • If gambling stops being fun, take a break and talk to someone you trust.
